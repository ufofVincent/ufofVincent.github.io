Hello, I’m Vincent.

Hello! My name is Vincent Han, an MCS graduate from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign specializing in machine learning and large language models. My interest in ML is quite broad: I am interested both in the theory of machine learning, especially topics such as the mathematics and statistics behind generative modeling and large language models, theoretical guarantees on reinforcement learning algorithms, and also in their application. I am currently working on formulating a geometric, manifold formulation of Deng et al.'s Generative Model via Drifting paper where I argue that the distributions a neural architecture can reach form a smooth Riemannian manifold. I further am interested in developing a general theory of drifting.


Aside from machine learning, I also have a keen interest in theoretical computer science and pure mathematics. I have conducted research in quantum computing and complexity classes. I also am strongly interested in differential and symplectic geometry in mathematics, while sometimes dabbling in reading topics in number theory, PDE's, and so on.


Contact me by emailing me! Also feel free to download my resume.
